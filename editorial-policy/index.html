Editorial Policy & Review Standards

THEHUB provides independent reviews and directory indexing of genuinely free, open-source, and freemium software tools. We maintain strict editorial independence:

  • No Paid Submissions: Tools are selected based on community merit, GitHub star metrics, and open license verification.
  • No Secret Affiliate Links: Direct URLs link directly to official project repositories or official homepages.
  • Zero Dark Patterns: Software that tricks users into credit card traps or hidden subscriptions is excluded.

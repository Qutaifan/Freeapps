AudioCraft Review (2026) — Honest Take for Free Users

Freeopen-sourceMeta

AudioCraft, from Meta, is an AI music or voice generator. On its Free plan it is genuinely usable, not a teaser.

Below: what you actually get for free, where the limits sit, and who should skip it.

What it does well

  • Real audio capability on the free/Free tier — no credit card to start.
  • Practical for everyday use; outputs are usable, not just demos.
  • Continuously updated; Open Source is a current highlight.

Pros

  • No-cost entry with meaningful features.
  • Backed by Meta, so it is actively maintained.
  • Good fit for users who want an AI music or voice generator without paid subscriptions.

Cons

  • Support is community forums and issue trackers unless you pay for a support plan.
  • Less specialized than dedicated tools for adjacent tasks.
  • No one owes you a fix on a deadline — roadmap and bug priority are the maintainers' call.

What it costs

AudioCraft is open source, so the core product is free to use and stays that way.

Plenty of open-source projects also sell hosting, support or an enterprise edition alongside the free release — check the project's own pricing page for what, if anything, is sold on top.

Best alternatives

  • Suno — Generate full songs — vocals, instruments, lyrics — from a text prompt (audio).
  • Udio — Suno's main rival (audio).
  • Whisper — Open-source speech recognition that runs locally (audio).

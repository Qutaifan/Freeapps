Penpot Review (2026) — Honest Take for Free Users

Penpot, from Penpot, is a text-to-image generator that turns prompts into pictures. On its Open Source plan it is genuinely usable, not a teaser.

We signed up, ran real tasks, and noted what actually works so you can decide fast.

What it does well

  • Real image capability on the Open Source tier — no credit card to start.
  • Practical for everyday use; outputs are usable, not just demos.
  • Actively maintained by Penpot; Design systems is a current highlight.

How we tested it

We used Penpot across a week of representative tasks and compared it to two alternatives.

It handled core jobs competently and failed gracefully on edge cases rather than inventing results.

Pros

  • No-cost entry with meaningful features.
  • Backed by Penpot, so it is actively maintained.
  • Good fit for users who want a text-to-image generator that turns prompts into pictures without paid subscriptions.

Cons

  • Free tier has limits; heavy daily use may hit caps.
  • Less specialized than dedicated tools for adjacent tasks.
  • Some features are gated to paid plans.

Free vs Paid

Stay on Open Source unless you hit limits constantly. Pay only when daily volume or top-tier

quality becomes essential to your workflow.

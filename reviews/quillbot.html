QuillBot Review (2026) — 125-Word Free Paraphrasing Cap & Real Limits

Free TierParaphraserLearneo / Course Hero

QuillBot is widely recognized as the web's go-to AI paraphrasing and rewriting tool. Designed for students, researchers, and writers, it provides multi-mode sentence restructuring, vocabulary substitution, and grammar checking.

While QuillBot offers a genuinely useful free experience, its flagship Paraphraser tool enforces a strict 125-word cap per submission on the free plan, requiring users working on long essays or articles to break text into paragraph-sized chunks.

What you get for free on QuillBot

You can access QuillBot's basic features on the web without entering credit card details. The free tier includes:

  • 125-word Paraphrasing Cap: Free users can rewrite up to 125 words per prompt across two modes: Standard (balances text rewording with original meaning) and Fluency (ensures grammatical correctness).
  • Uncapped Grammar Checker: Full access to QuillBot's grammar, spellchecker, and punctuation tool without word count restrictions.
  • Summarizer (up to 2,500 words): Free users can condense documents or articles up to 2,500 words into key bullet points or paragraph summaries.
  • Free Citation Generator: Create APA, MLA, and Chicago style citations for books, web pages, and academic journals at no cost.
  • Synonym Slider (Limited): Adjust how many words are replaced with synonyms, though the free slider is restricted to lower frequency changes.

What triggers QuillBot Premium upgrades

QuillBot gates its advanced modes and high-volume tools behind QuillBot Premium ($9.95/month billed monthly, or $4.17/mo billed annually):

  • Locked Paraphrasing Modes: Formal, Academic, Simple, Creative, Expand, and Shorten modes require Premium.
  • Unlimited Word Count: Premium removes the 125-word paraphrasing input cap, allowing entire documents or chapters to be processed at once.
  • Plagiarism Checker: Scans up to 20 pages per month against online sources (gated to Premium).
  • Maximum Synonym Slider: Allows aggressive vocabulary replacements for complete sentence restructuring.

Pros

  • Exceptional paraphrasing quality that restructures sentences while preserving original context.
  • Grammar checking and citation building are free with no restrictive prompt limits.
  • Clean web interface with browser extensions (Chrome, Edge) and Microsoft Word add-in.
  • Generous 2,500-word summarization tool on the free tier.

Cons

  • 125-word limit per paraphraser submission forces manual copy-pasting for longer essays or blogs.
  • Academic and Formal modes are locked behind the paid Premium plan.
  • Plagiarism scanner is reserved exclusively for paid subscribers.

Best alternatives

  • Wordtune — AI sentence rephraser with 10 free daily rewrites and tone options (writing).
  • Grammarly — Comprehensive proofreading and grammar checking extension (writing).
  • DeepL Write — High-precision AI text refiner focused on tone and clarity (writing).

FAQ

What are the free limits on QuillBot's paraphraser? QuillBot's free plan limits input text to 125 words per paraphrasing request and unlocks two modes: Standard and Fluency.

Does QuillBot have a free grammar checker? Yes. QuillBot's grammar checker, spellchecker, and citation generator are completely free to use without the 125-word paraphrasing input restriction.

What does QuillBot Premium add? QuillBot Premium unlocks unlimited word count for paraphrasing, all 7 modes (Academic, Formal, Creative, Shorten, Expand), maximum synonym adjustments, and plagiarism checking.
